validated for colour-vision deficiency on every Teams surface, light and dark

Project colours and day-type tints never share a cell — a holiday or full-leave day carries no project bar. That is what lets three saturated project hues coexist with five semantic day tints without either encoding becoming ambiguous. Three projects is the validated ceiling: a fourth categorical hue cannot clear the colour-vision separation floor against this set, so a fourth allocation should fold into an “Other” segment.
